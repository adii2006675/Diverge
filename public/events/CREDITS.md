- Sources are Wikimedia Commons files, downloaded at 960px wide on 2026-10-15 through Commons' `Special:FilePath`. Each row links to the file's Commons page, which has the full author, source and licence details.
- Licence names come from the Commons metadata at download time. CC BY and CC BY-SA images need attribution when shown publicly; the app does not display credits yet, so add an "Image credits" link before any public release.
- The six images marked **project-provided** were already in the repository. Their source and licence were not recorded, so verify them before any public release.
